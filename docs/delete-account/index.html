Delete your Hunt.it account

This page is provided by ghost.dev for users of the Hunt.it mobile app.

Fastest option: delete in the app

  1. Open Hunt.it and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar on the home screen.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

The deletion is permanent and begins immediately.

Request deletion online

If you cannot access the app, email the address below from the email used for your Hunt.it account. Use the subject “Hunt.it account deletion”. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion.

Start deletion request

Data deleted

Your authentication account, email, profile, display name, avatar selection, hosted rooms, participation, scores, submission records, and Hunt.it-stored challenge photos are deleted.

Data that may be retained

Safety reports may be retained without your account identifiers for up to 24 months when needed to investigate abuse, protect users, or comply with legal obligations. Backup and security logs may persist for a limited period before automatic expiry.

Timing

In-app deletion is immediate when the service is available. Verified email requests are normally completed within 7 days and no later than 30 days unless retention is legally required.
