Hunt.it Privacy Policy

Effective: August 1, 2026 · Developer: ghost.dev

Privacy Terms of Use Delete your account

Hunt.it is for people aged 18 and older. Do not create an account or use the app if you are under 18.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address, account identifier, display name, and selected avatar.
  • Gameplay data: rooms, participation, challenges, scores, game status, and timestamps.
  • Photos you submit for a scavenger-hunt challenge.
  • Safety data: blocked-player relationships and reports you submit.
  • Technical data necessary to authenticate requests, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

How we use and share data

We use account and gameplay data to authenticate you, run multiplayer rooms, calculate scores, show game history, prevent abuse, and provide support.

Challenge photos are sent to Google Gemini for automated verification against the current challenge. They are not made public by Hunt.it. Google processes the photo and verification prompt under the applicable Gemini API terms and privacy commitments. Hunt.it does not sell personal data or use challenge photos for advertising.

Supabase provides authentication, database, realtime, function, and private storage infrastructure for Hunt.it. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention

  • Challenge photos are deleted from Hunt.it private storage after the verification attempt. Interrupted uploads are also removed when the account is deleted.
  • Finished game records are normally deleted after 7 days.
  • Account and active gameplay data remain while the account is active or until no longer needed to provide the service.
  • Safety reports may be retained for up to 24 months to investigate abuse. Account identifiers on reports are removed when the related account is deleted.

Your choices

You can change your display name and avatar, report or block another player, and permanently delete your account in the app. You can also start an online deletion request from the account-deletion page.

Security and children

Hunt.it uses access controls, private photo storage, authenticated server functions, and encrypted network transport. No system is perfectly secure. Hunt.it is not directed to children and may only be used by people aged 18 or older.

You are responsible for display names, room names, photos, and other content you submit. Hunt.it may remove content, restrict rooms, suspend accounts, or preserve evidence when reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate reports, or comply with law.

Automated verification

Photo verification is automated and may be incorrect. Scores and availability are not guaranteed. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, dangerous, or illegal images.

Changes and availability

The service may change, experience interruptions, or be discontinued. Material policy changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
