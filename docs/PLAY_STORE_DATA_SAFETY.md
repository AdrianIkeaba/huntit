# Hunt.it Play Console data-safety worksheet

This is an implementation-aligned worksheet, not the final legal submission. Recheck every SDK and production configuration immediately before filing.

## Collected data

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info | Email address, display name, account ID, 18+ confirmation timestamp | Account management, authentication, multiplayer identity, eligibility | Supabase as service provider |
| Photos and videos | Challenge photo | Core gameplay and automated challenge verification | Supabase private storage; Google Gemini for verification |
| App activity | Rooms, participation, challenges, scores, game status | Multiplayer gameplay, history, fraud prevention | Supabase as service provider |
| User-generated content | Display names, room names, safety reports | Multiplayer features and moderation | Supabase as service provider |
| App information and performance | Service and security logs generated by infrastructure | Reliability, security, abuse prevention | Supabase and Google infrastructure as applicable |

## Handling

- Data is encrypted in transit.
- User accounts and associated data can be deleted in-app and through the public deletion-request page.
- Challenge photos are private and removed from Hunt.it storage after the verification attempt.
- Finished games are normally retained for 7 days.
- Safety reports are retained for up to 24 months; account identifiers are removed on account deletion.
- Data is not sold and is not used by Hunt.it for advertising.
